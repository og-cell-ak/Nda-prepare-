# NDA English research and exam-planning guide

Last reviewed: 9 October 2026

## Source hierarchy

1. **UPSC official examination notices and papers** are the source of truth for the scheme, syllabus, and past paper wording.
2. Use official question papers to identify recurring *skills and formats*, not to claim that any exact question will repeat.
3. Coaching sites and question banks can help identify practice patterns, but their labels, answer keys, and frequency claims must be cross-checked. They are not official UPSC sources.
4. Every item written specifically for this app is labelled original practice, not a previous-year question (PYQ).

## What the official scheme establishes

The 2025 NDA/NA notice states that the written examination includes Mathematics (300 marks) and General Ability Test (600 marks), for 900 written marks, plus SSB Test/Interview (900 marks). The GAT has English and General Knowledge sections; English is 200 marks and GK 400 marks in the notified scheme. Written papers are objective type. Always check the notice for the specific exam cycle because the current official notice controls.

The syllabus describes English as testing understanding of English and workmanlike use of words. The app therefore trains grammar and usage, vocabulary, meaning in context, sentence structure, and reading comprehension. Topic names used by coaching sites are helpful practice categories, but UPSC does not promise a fixed question count for each micro-topic.

## Skills to prepare

- Core grammar: parts of speech, subject–verb agreement, articles, pronouns, nouns, verbs, tense, auxiliaries, modals, voice, narration, conditionals, conjunctions, modifiers and punctuation.
- Vocabulary: synonyms, antonyms, word meaning in context, commonly confused words, phrasal verbs, idioms and phrases, one-word substitutions and spelling.
- Sentence skills: sentence completion, improvement, error identification, ordering/rearrangement, connectors and logical flow.
- Reading: passage comprehension, central idea, explicit detail, inference, tone and contextual meaning.
- Practice formats often used by preparation materials: fill in the blanks, cloze passages and mixed grammar/vocabulary sets. Treat the precise distribution as variable rather than guaranteed.
- SSB communication: self-introduction, listening, clear concise answers, describing a situation, explaining a decision, group discussion and interview practice. SSB assessment is broader than English alone.

## Forecasting what might appear

No honest source can guarantee next year's exact questions. Use a **coverage forecast**, not a prediction of exact items:

- **High priority, durable foundations:** agreement, tense and verb forms, articles, prepositions, pronouns, vocabulary in context, synonyms/antonyms, sentence correction and reading comprehension. These skills transfer across many question formats.
- **High priority, format rotation:** idioms/phrases, sentence ordering, fill-in-the-blank, connectors and cloze-style contextual choices. Practise them even if a particular recent paper appears to contain fewer examples.
- **Advanced coverage:** voice, narration, conditionals, inversion/fixed structures, modifiers, phrasal verbs, one-word substitutions, spelling and nuanced vocabulary. These add resilience if a future paper shifts difficulty.
- **Never forecast as certainty:** an exact number of questions, a guaranteed topic, or a claim that a past question will repeat.

For each new official paper, record date, question reference, skill, difficulty, answer source and confidence. Update priorities using several papers, not a single sitting. Do not confuse a question's popularity on coaching websites with official exam weight.

## Important limitation of the current app

The in-app question bank currently contains **70 original practice questions**, not a complete official PYQ archive and not enough by itself to claim complete preparation. No questions in this bank are labelled verified PYQs. The app is a foundation that should grow through verified paper-by-paper transcription and independent answer checking. Do not present it as a substitute for reading the official notification and papers.
