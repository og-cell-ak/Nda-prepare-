# NDA Prepare: suggested study plan

Designed for a learner starting English from near zero. Suggested baseline: 35–45 minutes daily, adjusted to the learner's schedule.

## Phase 1: Build the base (weeks 1–3)
- Learn sentence order, pronouns, nouns, be-verbs, articles, simple present/past/future and common prepositions.
- Read each example aloud and make three personal examples.
- Do 10–15 beginner questions daily; write down why each wrong option is wrong.

## Phase 2: Build accuracy (weeks 4–6)
- Study agreement, auxiliaries, modals, continuous/perfect tenses, conjunctions and common verb/adjective + preposition patterns.
- Start vocabulary cards: word, simple definition, Hindi meaning, synonym, antonym, one sentence and review date.
- Practise short passages, sentence correction, ordering, connectors and cloze-style questions.

## Phase 3: Exam-level application (weeks 7–10)
- Mix topics instead of practising only one category.
- Keep an error log: question, mistake type, correct rule, your corrected sentence.
- Take timed mini-tests, then review every wrong or guessed answer.
- Use official UPSC NDA GAT papers as the benchmark; keep official PYQs separate from original practice.

## Phase 4: Revision and resilience (weeks 11 onward)
- Rotate grammar, vocabulary, idioms, sentence skills and comprehension.
- Repeat weak-topic sets after 1 day, 3 days, 7 days and 14 days.
- Take mixed mocks under time limits and track accuracy by topic.
- Practise speaking for 2–5 minutes daily: introduction, an event, a reasoned opinion, a teamwork example and a goal.

## Daily template
1. 10 minutes: learn one rule.
2. 10 minutes: revise vocabulary.
3. 15 minutes: solve questions and read explanations.
4. 5 minutes: speak aloud and listen to your own clarity.
5. 5 minutes: update the error log.

## Readiness checks
- Can explain the rule behind an answer, not just remember the option.
- Accuracy remains stable on mixed sets, not only familiar questions.
- Can understand a short passage and summarise it in simple English.
- Can speak clearly and honestly without memorising a fake personality.
